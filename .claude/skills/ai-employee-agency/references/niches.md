# Niches

The best niches have high-value jobs, many urgent inbound calls, and owners who are on job sites instead of at a desk.

| Niche | Why it fits | Job-value search | Urgent calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roofing | Large tickets; storm and leak calls; crews on roofs | "roof repair cost <city>" / "roof replacement cost" (Angi, HomeAdvisor) | leaks, storm damage |
| HVAC | Emergency calls; seasonal spikes | "AC repair cost", "furnace repair cost" | no heat, no AC |
| Plumbing | Emergencies around the clock | "plumber cost per visit", "water heater replacement cost" | leaks, clogs, no hot water |
| Electrical | Owners on jobs; quote-based | "electrician cost per hour <city>" | outages, sparks |
| Garage doors | Urgent; mostly one-person shops | "garage door repair cost" | door stuck open |
| Pest control | Recurring plans; urgent first calls | "pest control cost", "termite treatment cost" | wasps, rodents |
| Dental | Valuable new patients; front desk often busy | "new patient value dental" (ADA or practice-management sources) | toothache, broken tooth |
| Med spa / aesthetics | High-value consultations; Instagram-driven | "botox cost <city>" | low |
| Auto repair / body shop | Phones ring while staff work | "<repair> cost" (RepairPal) | breakdowns |
| Law (personal injury, family) | One signed case is worth a lot | use consultation-to-retainer value, cited | arrests, accidents |
| Moving | Quote shopping; first to answer wins | "local movers cost <city>" | low |
| Landscaping / tree service | Owner in the field | "tree removal cost" | fallen trees |

Avoid: chains or franchises with central call centers, businesses that already run an AI or answering service (check for a chat widget, "24/7 live answering" or "powered by" on the site), and niches with regulated advice (law and medicine) unless the receptionist only does intake and never gives advice.

If the user doesn't name a niche, suggest roofing, HVAC and plumbing first: they have the clearest missed-call story.
