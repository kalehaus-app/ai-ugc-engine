# Outreach email

## Structure (under 120 words)
1. **Something specific about them**: one detail that proves you looked. For example, a review quote about not being able to reach them, or "your site says call for a quote, and you close at 5pm".
2. **The cost, framed as an estimate**: "Our rough estimate is that missed calls cost a shop your size around $X a month." Use the *likely* figure. Never present it as a fact about their books.
3. **The fix in one sentence**: an AI receptionist that answers 24/7 and texts you every lead.
4. **Proof they can try**: "I set one up already trained on {{business_name}}. Call {{test_number}} and hear it."
5. **A small ask**: "Worth a 10-minute look this week?"
6. **Signature with opt-out**: name, Kale Haus, physical mailing address, and "Not interested? Just reply 'no' and I won't email again."

## Subject lines (write three)
Keep them specific and honest, with no fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" prefixes. For example:
- "Missed calls at {{business_name}}"
- "Built a 24/7 receptionist for {{business_name}}"
- "Quick idea for {{business_name}}'s after-hours calls"

## Follow-up (send 3–4 days later, under 60 words)
Point back to the test number, add one new angle (such as weekend calls or storm season), and end with the same small ask.

## Rules
- **CAN-SPAM (US):** no misleading subject or From line, include a valid physical postal address, include a clear way to opt out, and honor opt-outs within 10 business days. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- Only email addresses that are publicly listed for business contact. Never guess or scrape personal addresses.
- Don't send it. Write it, and create a Gmail **draft** only if the user asks and a connector is available.
- Don't claim results you haven't had: no "our clients see 3× bookings" without real data.

## Optional phone or DM script (for the user to use themselves)
"Hi, is this the owner? I'm {{user_name}} with Kale Haus. I ran a quick look at {{business_name}} and estimated you're missing around $X a month in calls that go unanswered. I built a demo AI receptionist trained on your business. Can I text you the number so you can hear it?"
